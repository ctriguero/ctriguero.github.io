About

I am a physicist and teacher. My research background is in computational and statistical physics, with papers on phase transitions, porous materials, magnetism and molecular simulation. Today I teach physics from GCSE to A level, and I enjoy showing students that an equation, a graph and an experiment can tell the same story.

What I teach

Mechanics

Forces, Newton’s laws, motion, graphs and energy.

Waves and optics

Waves, light, lenses and interference.

Electricity and magnetism

Circuits, fields and electromagnetic induction.

Thermal physics and materials

Heat, gases and the properties of matter.

Aerospace principles

Flight, lift and drag, rockets and orbits.

Relativity

Time dilation, length contraction and mass–energy equivalence.

Quantum mechanics

Photons, wave–particle duality and atomic energy levels.

Practical work

Planning, measuring and evaluating experiments.
