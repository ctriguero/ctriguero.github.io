About me

I am a physicist and teacher. My research background is in computational and statistical physics, with papers on phase transitions, porous materials, magnetism and molecular simulation. Today I teach physics from GCSE to A level, and I enjoy showing students that an equation, a graph and an experiment can tell the same story.

What I teach

  • Mechanics: forces, Newton’s laws, motion, graphs and energy
  • Waves, light and optics
  • Electricity and magnetism
  • Thermal physics and the properties of materials
  • Aerospace principles: flight, lift and drag, rockets and orbits
  • Relativity: time dilation, length contraction and mass–energy equivalence
  • Quantum mechanics: photons, wave–particle duality and atomic energy levels
  • Practical work: planning, measuring and evaluating experiments
